Information may be used, where applicable, to:

V-Tik should not claim to perform a use that is not actually implemented. For example, personalized recommendations, analytics, or marketing should be described here only if those functions are used.

5. Videos and public content

Content you publish may be visible to other people depending on how V-Tik is designed and configured. Public videos, usernames, captions, comments, and profile information may be copied, shared, or viewed outside the site by other users.

Only post content you have permission to share. Avoid including your home address, private contact details, school or workplace details, or other information you do not want to make public. If V-Tik supports deleting or editing posts, use those controls when available; deletion from the site may not remove copies others have already made.

6. Cookies and technical data

This template does not assume that V-Tik currently uses advertising cookies or analytics. Confirm the actual configuration before publishing. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

To make a privacy request, contact V-Tik using the contact details published below. The site owner should add a real, monitored contact address before publishing and provide a reasonable process for verifying and responding to requests. You may also be able to change browser cookie settings or remove content through site controls, where available.

10. Young users

V-Tik should be used in line with applicable age requirements and local laws. Do not knowingly collect personal information from children in ways that require parental consent or other safeguards without putting those safeguards in place. If you believe a young person has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the site owner using the details below.

Before launch, the site owner should set an appropriate minimum age, create age-appropriate safety practices, and make sure account, messaging, and content features follow the laws that apply in the places where V-Tik is offered.
