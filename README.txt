V-TIK STARTER APP
==================

1. Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
2. Demo videos are already inside the videos folder.
3. Use the search bar, likes, saves, comments, share and Follow buttons.
4. The Create button previews a video selected from your computer.

This is a front-end demo. Real online video uploads, user accounts,
databases, payments, advertising and creator earnings require a backend
and hosting/storage service.
